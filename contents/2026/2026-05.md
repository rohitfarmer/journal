## 2026-05-28
tags: biking

Today was the first day I biked to work. I had been thinking about it ever since I bought the bike more than a month ago. It was fun. I dropped Elsa off at the school bus and walked with Eva to her daycare, then biked on the Bethesda Trolley Trail to work. It took me 27 minutes, a little over what the maps showed in the beginning, and I burned 287 calories. I will not be able to ride every day, but I will ride as much as I can over the summer. Especially on the days when I don't have early morning meetings.

## 2026-05-18
tags: camping, outdoors

From May 15 to 17, we went on our first family camping trip of the year with our friends. It was at the Rocky Gap State Park where we booked a cabin and a tent site. Sibyl and my friend's wife are not fans of the outdoors, and they preferred to stay in a cabin rather than in our tents. But since the weather was nice and we had so much fun, they decided to spend the second night with us in our tents. Kids had a blast on the hammocks, on the beach, and on our paddleboard and kayak. Since it was a week before the Memorial Day weekend, the beach facilities were not officially open, so there were no lifeguards, boat rentals, or beach shacks. For that reason, there were very few people, and we had the whole beach almost to ourselves, with probably five other families and a youth group. 

This was our second time camping at Rocky Gap State Park. The first time was in 2023, which did not go well. We met a thunderstorm, had to sleep in the car, and lost Elsa for 5 minutes at the playground. So some traumatizing memories were going back to the same places. But we had so much fun this time that I think it made up for all the past traumas.

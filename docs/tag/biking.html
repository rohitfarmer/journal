2026-04-21

🔗
  • biking

This year, we are focusing on cycling/biking, and during the Spring/Easter break, we managed to get a bike for each of us, along with a bike rack for my car. Since then, on the weekends, we go to the Beach Drive to ride our bikes. I went there by myself and rode 8.5 miles the first day, and on the second riding day, I rode 10 miles on the C&O Canal Towpath.

The C&O Canal towpath is a popular place to go bike touring/packing in the US, and since it's in Maryland, I want to tour the entire length of 184 miles from DC to Cumberland. I am thinking of riding 40 to 50 miles a day and completing the length in 4 to 5 days. But right now, I do not have the endurance to ride that long continuously for multiple days, and I also do not have all the accessories that I will need for my bike to go touring. So, I am giving myself this year, or at least the whole of spring and summer, to prepare, and will try to tour either in the fall this year or the spring next year.

I posted on Mastodon about resuming biking after 13 years, and I received a great response from the community.

We have been eagerly waiting for spring for a variety of reasons, but one thing that we want to achieve in 2026 is for everyone to learn how to ride a bike - at least Elsa, if not Eva. And Today the weather was nice, and I took her out in our courtyard to teach her how to bike without support wheels. She got it in just two hours and was able to ride without me supporting her or falling too many times. She still has to work on her turns and breaks. But I am so impressed by how quickly she picked it up. She has really good balance. She also picked up hoverboarding and roller skating pretty quickly.

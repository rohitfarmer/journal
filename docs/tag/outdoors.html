Today, we went for a daddy-daughter boating day on the lake at Black Hill Regional Park. Black Hill is one of my favorite places to go paddling. I decided to try the rowboat I had never tried before. So, it was a different experience from kayaking or SUP because when you are rowing, you are going backward, and navigation is a bit difficult. I also didn't like the whole rowing motion, which is very different from the one on a gym rowing machine. After 1 hour of rowing, the kids decided to switch to a pedal boat, which we did, and we really enjoyed the extra hour we spent on the water.

So all in all, we spent two hours boating on two different types of boats and had a lot of fun. Elsa tried to help me propel and steer both types of boats, and Eva was chilling, having her snacks and juice. They loved the whole experience so much that they want to do it again next week. So we will see.

2026-05-18

From May 15 to 17, we went on our first family camping trip of the year with our friends. It was at the Rocky Gap State Park where we booked a cabin and a tent site. Sibyl and my friend's wife are not fans of the outdoors, and they preferred to stay in a cabin rather than in our tents. But since the weather was nice and we had so much fun, they decided to spend the second night with us in our tents. Kids had a blast on the hammocks, on the beach, and on our paddleboard and kayak. Since it was a week before the Memorial Day weekend, the beach facilities were not officially open, so there were no lifeguards, boat rentals, or beach shacks. For that reason, there were very few people, and we had the whole beach almost to ourselves, with probably five other families and a youth group.

This was our second time camping at Rocky Gap State Park. The first time was in 2023, which did not go well. We met a thunderstorm, had to sleep in the car, and lost Elsa for 5 minutes at the playground. So some traumatizing memories were going back to the same places. But we had so much fun this time that I think it made up for all the past traumas.

  • Today, I went for a walk at Cabin John Park, checked out the campgrounds, and walked along the creek. It was cold, but sunny, beautiful weather for a day out. I have been stuck at home since the beginning of the year, first because of illness and then because of bad weather. I am glad that I decided to go out today; it was therapeutic. I also managed to make a small video for my words and trails Instagram account. I never regret the time spent outdoors.

  • I am slowly transferring my notes from Simple Note to Joplin on my phone and home desktop. I knew about Joplin, but never seriously tried it. Now I have figured out how to synchronize notes using Dropbox and enable end-to-end encryption (E2EE). E2EE is what attracted me to give it serious thought, as I am trying to minimize the amount of my data that goes to tech companies. Joplins E2EE is device-to-device based and synced through the system of your choice; in my case, it's Dropbox. Even the folder on Dropbox is encrypted at rest. The only downside is that I can't share the notes or invite collaborations with my current setup. But other than that, I am liking it so far.

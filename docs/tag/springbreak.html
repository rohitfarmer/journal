2026-03-29

🔗
  • springbreak

It's the start of spring break, and this weekend we spent at Yokum Vacationland in West Virginia. It was just a one-night stay at Yokum's motel right in front of the Seneca Rocks. It was still cold, but bright and sunny. The 3-hour drive to Seneca Rocks was mesmerizing. It was our first time in West Virginia, and we loved it. Seneca Rocks is a 960-foot rock formation that is very popular among East Coast rock climbers. We went for a mini-hike since Eva couldn't walk too far, and spent some time hammocking next to the river at the Snowbird Princess Campground, which is part of Yokum Vacationland.

This time, we used our newly bought Sawyer water filter to refill our Nalgene bottles as and when required. So unlike before, we saved some disposable plastic bottles from going into the trash. I bought the Sawyer during the latest REI sale, which is something I had wanted to do for quite some time.

Yokum organized an Easter egg hunt for the kids. Both Elsa and Eva participated, and Eva won the prize for collecting the most eggs in her age group. Almost all the people who participated in the Easter Egg hunt were locals from nearby places, and surprisingly, among over 100 people, we were the only 4 people of color. Most men were wearing t-shirts or hoodies with crosses and American flags, and one family was all dressed in Confederate flags. So it was definitely a very conservative crowd. However, we did not feel uncomfortable, nor did anyone else say anything to us.

I also noticed that most people in that area must be blue-collar workers, since everyone was wearing Carhartt. I love Carhartt products, so I was happy to see that. I also saw a bunch of cars with big ham radio antennas. So, I am assuming that it is also quite popular there for good reason, as cell service was almost absent. Sibyl and I are on different networks, and neither of us had any connectivity.

Overall, we had a really great time!
